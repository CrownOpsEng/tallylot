- The asset snapshot is stored in `03_analysis/reconciliation/baseline_asset_snapshot.csv`.
- The reconciliation table is stored in `03_analysis/reconciliation/baseline_exchange_reconciliation.csv`.
- This is an internal CoinTracking consistency check only. It does not by itself prove that each external exchange or wallet was synced through the same cutoff.
- Source-level ledger coverage is summarized in `03_analysis/reconciliation/baseline_source_activity.csv`.
- The only negative balance in `Current Balance` is **CAD -15,654.23**.
- `baseline_cad_flow_by_type.csv` shows why: CAD bought **34,215.69**, CAD sold **49,869.92**, and CAD fees **156.26**.
- CAD deposits and withdrawals recorded in the ledger net to zero; the negative ending CAD balance is driven by net trade activity and CAD fees.
- The current documented interpretation is that this row belongs to an intentional general CAD tracking account, not an omitted exchange-side cash ledger leg.
- That means the negative CAD row is a real ledger state inside CoinTracking, not display noise.
- It is tracked as **FIAT-001** in `03_analysis/issues/issue_log.csv` and is currently closed unless conflicting data appears later.

CRA recordkeeping standard:

- CRA says records must be reliable, complete, and supported by documents: <https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/keeping-records/your-responsibilities-requirements-associated-records-law-requires-you-keep.html>
